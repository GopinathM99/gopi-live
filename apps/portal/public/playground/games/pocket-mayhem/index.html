THE VERY SHORT FIELD GUIDE

Make a little trouble.

  1. Move, don’t aim. Your robot shoots the nearest toy automatically. Use WASD, arrows, or the thumbstick.
  2. Dash through danger. Space or Shift makes you briefly invincible and smashes toys you pass through.
  3. Collect the blue batteries. Level up, pause the action, and choose one of three upgrades. Lime crates hide batteries and repairs.
  4. Unleash Overdrive. Recycling toys charges it. Press F or the star button for nine seconds of boosted firepower and a healing blast.
  5. Read the wind-up. Red marks announce incoming attacks. Bosses arrive at 2:30, 5:00, and 8:00. Beat the final boss to win—or go endless.
